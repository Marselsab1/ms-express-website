Our process

How to Work With CLC

Four steps from the first message to a finished yard. Here's exactly what happens.

Fill in the form or call (513) 580-6732. Tell us the town, roughly what the property looks like now, and what you want done. Photos help.

Carson comes out and looks at the job in person. You get a fixed price off what he's actually seen, plus any questions answered on the spot.

You pick a day that works and we confirm it. If anything changes on our end you'll hear it from us first, not find out on the day.

The crew stays until the job is finished and cleans up behind itself. Before we leave you'll get a few tips on keeping the work looking right.
