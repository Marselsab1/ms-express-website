Who you're hiring

You'll be dealing with Carson.

CLC Outdoor Services is a lawn care and landscaping company working out of the Cincinnati area. Carson runs it, quotes it, and is usually on the truck for it.

That's most of the pitch. One person knows your property, walks it with you before any number gets quoted, and answers the phone afterwards. No dispatcher, no crew you've never met turning up unannounced.

The work runs the full year: weekly mowing and sharp bed edging through the season, fertilizing, aeration and overseeding to keep turf thick, mulch and bed work in spring, and property cleanups once the leaves come down. Residential and commercial both.
